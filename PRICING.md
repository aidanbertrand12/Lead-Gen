## ROI framing for the pitch
- One new MSA with an operator is often worth **$250k–$1M+ a year** in work for a service company. The package pays for itself if it wins **a single new account**.
- Operators prequalify vendors online. A 2012-era website without HSE information gets a company screened out before anyone calls.
- After-hours RFQs: about 30–40% of web inquiries arrive outside business hours. The AI assistant captures them instead of losing them.

## How the audit feeds the pitch
Each lead in `data/leads.csv` has a `pitch` column that maps findings to the line items above:
- *Website down, not mobile-friendly, no HTTPS, Flash, old copyright year* → **#2 Website rebuild**
- *No chat, no form, no quote call-to-action* → **#4 AI assistant + RFQ intake**
- *No analytics, only a mailto link, no CRM* → **#5 CRM + automation**
- *Everyone* → **#3 AI lead-gen engine** (this is the revenue story)
